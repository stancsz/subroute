# Badlands mark

The original Mesa B is the shared, single-color Badlands Labs mark. Canonical
geometry is in `badlands-mark.svg`, with a 32×32 view box and two open counters.
Use currentColor for headers and footers. Keep the full company or product name
as selectable text alongside it; the mark alone is appropriate for favicons and
compact navigation. Do not round, stretch or add gradients to its geometry.

Headers use 30–32px, compact navigation 24px and footers 20px. The favicon uses
a black mark on a white square backing, which remains visible on dark tabs.
Lossless rendered 16/20/24/32px proofs at DPR1/2 in both polarities were inspected
by the image-capable GPT-6.1 Sol advisor. Company desktop/mobile header/footer
lockups were also inspected. The same SVG is packaged in the company site and
inlined in Subroute and the gateway console to avoid asset-route changes.
